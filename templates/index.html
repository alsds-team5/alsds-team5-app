About this model & assumptions

The Huff Gravity Model estimates the probability that customers from each Census Block Group (CBG) will visit a proposed store rather than a competitor. Attraction is proportional to floor area and inversely proportional to travel distance (raised to a calibrated exponent).

Inputs: proposed location (lat/lon), business category (NAICS), and store floor area (sq ft).

Limitations: results depend on data availability and calibration quality. Treat outputs as estimates for comparative analysis, not precise predictions.
