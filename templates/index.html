What this system does

This tool helps business owners find the best retail location in Worcester, MA. It uses the Huff Gravity Model to estimate how likely customers are to visit your store compared to nearby competitors.

💬
What you provide

Your business type (e.g., "hardware store"), a location on the map, and your proposed store size in square meters.

📊
What you get back

Predicted market share, estimated customer visits, nearby competitor names and distances, and a side-by-side comparison if you test more than one location.
